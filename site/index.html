02 / Our point of view

Automation should
show its work.

These tools grew out of real operational problems: exhausted disks, unhealthy containers, risky changes, and the need to know exactly what happened.

Read the architecture ↗

Observe before acting.

Give execution a boundary.

Verify the result independently.
